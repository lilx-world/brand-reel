03 Customer story · Spec edit

Willow — Customer Story

Story from podcast · Edit · AI visuals

A long Riverside podcast recording turned into a 72-second customer story for Willow, a voice transcription app. No script was provided. I found the story in the conversation and cut it down. The podcast is the only live footage. Every other shot is an AI-generated image or clip, made to fit what the speaker says.

◆ For working with little oversight

Story judgment with no brief to lean on. I chose the strongest lines, set the order and built the visuals around them. This is the work you'd hand me when the raw footage arrives with only a short creative note.

04 Educational explainer · Motion graphics

The Lacemaker

Image sourcing · Motion graphics · Edit · Rights log

An art-history video on why Vermeer painted the front of The Lacemaker out of focus. The client sent a script and voiceover only. I sourced every image, built all the motion graphics, and edited the film.

◆ For your explainers and brand content

Explainer motion graphics, built to the voiceover. Slow push-ins move across the painting like a magnifying glass. A split screen sets the painting beside a modern shallow-focus image, and quiet serif titles keep the focus on the art. Every asset has a logged source and licence. Two images are AI-generated, and the log says so. See the media log (PDF).
